# Changelog

## 0.2.0 (Beta)

Still a beta. I tested it thoroughly with automated tests (every form of Iron's and Ars spells with overcharge, the question, the command and the config screen in all test groups, the chain with Ars 'n Spells), but I do not play with Iron's Spells 'n Spellbooks or Ars Nouveau myself, so your reports are the real test. Please report bugs on the [issue tracker](https://github.com/Drehverschluss/RunelessRPG-Mana-Bridge/issues) and add the output of `/runelessrpg_bridge status`.

- **Requires RunelessRPG 0.4.1 or newer**: it adds the methods for overcharge on spells of other mods.
- **Added: a question at the first login which mana system should lead the shared pool**, for the owner of a singleplayer world (also the LAN host) or an operator on a server, while Iron's or Ars is connected. Every button shows the base max mana and regeneration it brings, read from the real configs. Every answer turns the new server option `askForManaSystem` off; "Decide later" asks again at the next login. Clients without the bridge get a chat line with clickable values. Packs that ship their config should set `askForManaSystem = false`.
- **Added: command `/runelessrpg_bridge manaSystem [<id>]`** to show the leading pool and the possible values (every player) or to change it at runtime without losing mana or debt (operators, the console and the owner of a singleplayer or LAN world). Tab completion offers only values that can lead right now.
- **Changed: `manaSystem` and `manaDisplay` are choice buttons in the config screen** with readable names and a tooltip for every value; values that do not work right now stay in the file and show as not available. `displayUnit` shows readable names (Automatic, Leading mod, RunelessRPG mana) and a tooltip for every value. The config file keeps the mod IDs and AUTO, LEADER and RUNELESS.
- **Added: `runelessrpg:overcharge` works on Iron's Spells 'n Spellbooks and Ars Nouveau spells** with the same rules as on Spell Engine spells: they cost more (after the lower limit of Mana Cost) and deal more damage and healing (RunelessRPG's `overchargeDamageFactor` and `overchargeAffectsHealing`). With overcharge 0.2 an Iron's Fireball costs 14.4 instead of 12 RunelessRPG mana and deals 10 % more damage. New server option `applyOvercharge` per adapter (default `true`).
- **Added: summons, zones and runes of Iron's and Ars spells keep the overcharge bonus they were made with** (`overchargeAffectsSummons`), also after the chunk is saved and loaded; Iron's projectiles keep the bonus of the cast that fired them. Only spells the player pays through the bridge get the bonus: no scrolls, Ars turrets or runes from Runic Chalk, no spells that Ars 'n Spells pays; damage over time and effects stay as they are. Creative players pay nothing and get the bonus, as with Spell Engine.
- **Added: the overcharge line** ("Overcharge: +10% damage") in the tooltip of the active Iron's spell and of Ars casting items; all converted cost displays include the surcharge. The status command shows the player's overcharge with the cost and bonus factor, and `applyOvercharge`.
- **Fixed: in the chain with Ars 'n Spells on `ars_primary`**, Iron's own regeneration no longer adds to the shared pool (`disableOwnRegen` covers it now), and Ars 'n Spells no longer cancels Iron's spells with `RESOURCE_CHANGED` while RunelessRPG leads (a rounding difference in the conversion).
- **Fixed: the Ars spell book and the converted Ars costs show what Ars 'n Spells really takes** while it pays Ars spells itself: without Mana Cost and the overcharge surcharge of the bridge, and without the overcharge line. A change of its mode (also `/ans mode set`) reaches the clients within a second.
- **Fixed:** a known bridge that is also listed in `conflictingMods` is named once in the warning, the chat message and the status.
- **Corrected in the docs: Ars 'n Spells pays Ars spells itself in every mode of its mana unification, also on `separate`.** Mana Cost, the debt rules and overcharge of the bridge do not apply to these Ars spells (this was already so in 0.1.0; the 0.1.0 docs were wrong about `separate`). For every function of the bridge with Iron's and Ars spells, set `enable_mana_unification = false` in `ars_n_spells-server.toml`; Ars 'n Spells keeps its other functions.
- **Corrected in the docs:** while Ars leads, Ars *can* cut the mana to the max without gear on relog and respawn; it depends on the tick and does not happen every time.
- Clients and servers with 0.1.0 can still connect to 0.2.0: the new payloads use the optional channel. A 0.1.0 client gets the question as a chat line and shows costs without the overcharge surcharge (the server still takes it); a 0.1.0 server has no question and no overcharge for Iron's and Ars spells.

## 0.1.0 (Beta)

First public version, released as a beta. I tested it thoroughly with automated tests, but I do not play with Iron's Spells 'n Spellbooks or Ars Nouveau myself, so your reports are the real test. Please report bugs on the [issue tracker](https://github.com/Drehverschluss/RunelessRPG-Mana-Bridge/issues) and add the output of `/runelessrpg_bridge status`.

- **One shared mana pool for RunelessRPG, Iron's Spells 'n Spellbooks and Ars Nouveau**: Iron's spells, its Mana potion, Gluttony and the `/mana` command, Ars spells of every form, the Ring of Jumping, the Repairing perk, the Void Jar and Ars addons that add or remove mana use the same mana as Spell Engine spells. 1 Iron's mana and 1 Ars mana = 0.2 RunelessRPG mana by default, set automatically from the base values of the mods. Iron's and Ars are both optional.
- **The pack chooses which mod leads the pool** with `manaSystem`: `"runelessrpg"` (default), `"irons_spellbooks"` or `"ars_nouveau"`. The leading mod keeps its own base mana, regeneration and rules for respawn and death; while Ars leads, its progress, gear and familiars work as without this mod. The mode can be changed at runtime in every direction without losing mana or debt; if the chosen mod cannot lead (disabled, self test, another bridge), RunelessRPG leads with a warning that names the reason.
- **The same gear gives the same share of the pool in every mode**, only the regeneration follows the leading mod. Gear of the other mods counts for the leading pool: Iron's armor, rings, spell books and upgrade orbs, Ars' Mana Boost and Mana Regen enchantments, armor tiers, perks, curios and the Mana Regeneration potion, and RunelessRPG's enchantments, affixes and gear. Flat values are converted (+25 RunelessRPG max mana becomes +125 Iron's or Ars mana), percent values stay percent.
- **Ars progress counts too**: learned glyphs and the spell book tier raise the shared max mana and regeneration while Ars does not lead (`bridgeProgression`), and mana reserved by Ars familiars is taken from the shared max (`applyFamiliarReserve`).
- **Costs and debt**: Iron's and Ars spell costs follow `runelessrpg:mana_cost` (with its lower limit and Spell Infinity) and the debt rules of Spell Engine spells (DEBT modes, Borrowed Power and its backlash), in every mode; Ars' own discounts and cost events still count first. Everything that comes into the pool, also the leading mod's own regeneration, pays the debt back first.
- **Every player chooses the mana bar** (the leading mod's, RunelessRPG's, Iron's, Ars' or all) and the unit of every displayed amount. Iron's spell tooltips, the spell wheel, the Inscription Table, max mana and regeneration on Iron's and Ars gear, Ars' Rings of Discount and the cost bar of Ars' spell book are converted. RunelessRPG's bar shows while you are in mana debt, Iron's and Ars bars also show while you hold a casting item of another mod, and Ars' bar flashes red for every failed cast of any mod (`forwardInsufficientFlash`). Ars' bar updates at once when other mods change the pool.
- **Spell Mana Leech works on direct hits of Iron's and Ars spells**, with one shared counter for all mods. Two entity tags change which Iron's hits count; Ars' lasting zones (Linger, Wall and Orbit by default, option `leechZoneGlyphs`), runes, turrets and summons do not count.
- **Ars 'n Spells works in a chain** (`chainWithArsNSpells`): in its mode `iss_primary` only the Iron's adapter runs, in `ars_primary` only the Ars adapter, and Ars 'n Spells keeps Ars ↔ Iron's; changes of its mode with `/ans mode set` are noticed without a restart. Its mode `separate` keeps all functions of both mods and of this bridge.
- **Other mana bridges** (Ars Mana Bridge, Mana Transform, Mana Unifier, Unified Mana) are detected: the affected adapter turns off with a warning in the log and a chat message for operators, but only while the other bridge's mana function for that mod is on. A self test at startup turns an adapter off if Iron's or Ars changes, instead of breaking the game.
- **New command `/runelessrpg_bridge status`** for bug reports: versions, leading pool, adapters, other bridges, the important config values and your own mana, with a click that copies everything as English text. Every player can use it for themselves; operators and the console can name a player.
- The config keeps the sections of Iron's and Ars while they are not installed. Clients without this mod can still join a server with it, and clients with it can join servers without it.
- While Ars leads, Ars' own rules apply on death and respawn: the mana of the moment of death stays, and mana above the max without gear is cut on relog and respawn.
